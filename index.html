About Us

Who We Are

A Sweet Spot for Every Celebration Fun. Activities. Food.

Candy World Drinks N' Treats is your favorite candy store and food truck. Our unique take on sweets and treats, infused with creativity and candy, is a delight to guests at every event we're honored to be part of.

We specialize in concession treats infused with creativity and candy, served through a customer-centric, immersive experience.

Book us for birthday parties, student events, corporate events, outdoor festivals and carnivals. The fun doesn't stop when night falls: our LED-covered, custom-wrapped trailer glows in the dark to keep the party going!
